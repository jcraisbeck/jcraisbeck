I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Manning College of Information and Computing Sciences at the University of Massachusetts Amherst. Before beginning my Ph.D., I worked at the Center for Systems Biology at Massachusetts General Hospital under Professor Hakho Lee. I received my B.S. in Mathematics from the University of Massachusetts Lowell.

My research interest is in the phenomenon of machine learning: how machines learn, how different machines learn differently, and the sets of possible solutions. I view learning through the lens of Reinforcement Learning, the "most general subfield" of machine learning. For more, please see my research statement.

I am always looking for collaborators and interested in working with motivated students.
