Finding the Frame — RLC 2026

Poster: Learning & Remembering: Differential Expressivity Constrains Learning.

We argue that learning has two ingredients — a learning rule that decides how an agent would like to change its decisions, and a memory rule that realizes that change in parameters — and that the alignment between the two upper-bounds how much an agent can improve. We introduce differential expressivity, the tangent space of the architecture in function space, as the natural object for measuring that alignment.
